Good questions

How do I prepare for fine line and minimal?

We tell you when you book, so you arrive knowing what to expect. The same approach applies to custom tattoo designs and aftercare guidance.

Do you plan tattoo studio sessions around my needs?

Yes. We listen first and shape the session around what you want and how you feel. This applies to custom tattoo designs, sterile single-use needles and the rest of our tattoo studio work.

Can I bring a friend to body piercing?

Tell us when you book and we will make room, where the session allows. It is the same for sterile single-use needles as it is for custom tattoo designs.

How do I book custom tattoo designs?

Message or call, tell us what you would like and choose a time that suits you. That goes for cover-ups and reworks and fine line and minimal too.

Can I ask about sterile single-use needles before booking?

Of course. Message us and we will explain what is involved and how long it takes. Ask us about body piercing or aftercare guidance and the answer is the same.
