Good questions

Can I place an advance order for bakery and cake shop?

Yes. Tell us the day and what you need and we will confirm what we can make. The same approach applies to custom cakes and home delivery.

Do you cater to dietary needs for eggless options?

Tell us your needs before you order and the team will explain what is possible. This applies to custom cakes, eggless options and the rest of our bakery and cake shop work.

What goes into pastries and cupcakes?

Ask us when you order. The team is happy to describe ingredients and how it is made. It is the same for eggless options as it is for custom cakes.

Can I take custom cakes away?

Yes. Ask about takeaway when you order and we will pack it carefully. That goes for pastries and cupcakes and breads and cookies too.

Do you take large orders for breads and cookies?

Yes. Tell us the date and numbers and we will say what we can do. Ask us about wedding and party orders or home delivery and the answer is the same.

Want Pastries And Cupcakes Today?

Tell Sweet Crumbs what you fancy: custom cakes, pastries and cupcakes or something else. We will answer with what is ready and how to order.
