Good questions

What should I prepare before general pest control?

A clear path to the work area and a few details about the problem are usually enough; we will tell you if more is needed. Ask us about mosquito control or pre construction treatment and the answer is the same.

Do you explain the pest control work before starting?

Always. We describe each step, the materials and the cost, and begin only when you are comfortable. The same approach applies to general pest control and pre construction treatment.

Can I ask questions after bed bug removal?

Yes. Message us any time after the job and we will answer or come back to check. This applies to general pest control, termite treatment and the rest of our pest control work.

Do you handle small pest control jobs too?

Yes. Small and large jobs get the same care, and we quote both in the same clear way. It is the same for termite treatment as it is for general pest control.

How do you charge for rodent control?

We quote by the job and tell you what is included, so the final bill matches what we agreed. That goes for bed bug removal and rodent control too.
