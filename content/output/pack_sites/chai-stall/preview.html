Good questions

What goes into pakoda and samosa?

Ask us when you order. The team is happy to describe ingredients and how it is made. That goes for pakoda and samosa and cutting chai too.

Can I take kadak masala chai away?

Yes. Ask about takeaway when you order and we will pack it carefully. Ask us about cold coffee and lemon tea or takeaway cups and the answer is the same.

Do you take large orders for cutting chai?

Yes. Tell us the date and numbers and we will say what we can do. The same approach applies to kadak masala chai and takeaway cups.

How fresh is the chai stall food?

We make it each day and tell you honestly if something is not available. This applies to kadak masala chai, bun maska and toast and the rest of our chai stall work.

Can I ask what is best today in cold coffee and lemon tea?

Of course. Ask the team and they will point you to what is freshest. It is the same for bun maska and toast as it is for kadak masala chai.

Ready To Order Kadak Masala Chai?

Tell Chai stall what you fancy: kadak masala chai, pakoda and samosa or something else. We will answer with what is ready and how to order.
