Good questions

How do I prepare for follow up guidance?

We tell you what to do when you book, so you arrive knowing what to expect. This applies to easy appointments, time for questions and the rest of our clinic and medical work.

Can a family member come with me for easy appointments?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will make sure there is room. It is the same for time for questions as it is for easy appointments.

What if I am unsure about clear fees?

Ask us. We explain the options calmly and never rush your decision. That goes for plain explanations and follow up guidance too.

How do I book easy appointments?

Call or message us, say what you need and choose a time that suits you. Ask us about clear fees or calm waiting area and the answer is the same.

What should I bring to a clinic and medical visit?

Any earlier reports or notes, and a list of your questions; we will tell you if anything else is needed. The same approach applies to easy appointments and calm waiting area.

Need Clinic And Medical Advice?

Tell Clinics, hospitals and medical about your concern: easy appointments, plain explanations or something close. We will answer with the next step and a time.
