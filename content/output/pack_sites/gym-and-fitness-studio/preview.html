Good questions

Can I bring a friend to fitness assessment?

Tell us when you book and we will make room, where the session allows. It is the same for cardio zone as it is for strength floor.

How do I book strength floor?

Message or call, tell us what you would like and choose a time that suits you. That goes for personal training and group classes too.

Can I ask about cardio zone before booking?

Of course. Message us and we will explain what is involved and how long it takes. Ask us about fitness assessment or open slots and the answer is the same.

What should I bring to a gym and fitness studio session?

Comfortable clothes and any questions you have; we will tell you if anything else is useful. The same approach applies to strength floor and open slots.

Can I change my time for personal training?

Yes. Tell us as early as you can and we will offer the nearest free slot. This applies to strength floor, cardio zone and the rest of our gym and fitness studio work.

Ready To Try Strength Floor?

Tell Gym and fitness studio what you would like: strength floor, personal training or something close. We will answer with options and a time.
